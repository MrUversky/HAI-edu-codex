# Structured Inputs

## Core objective
Design a workshop experience that helps mixed-function participants understand how agent systems work, touch the mechanics themselves, and leave with one realistic next step for their own work.

## Success conditions
- participants do something with their hands, not only listen
- the lesson demonstrates agents/workflows, not just generic prompting
- the wow moment leads to understanding and transfer, not only surprise
- participants leave with one concrete next step
- the session creates a bridge to deeper work: pilot, workshop, or program

## Audience realities
- mixed functions: operations, HR, product, sales, maybe finance
- mixed AI maturity: some already use AI, some are intimidated
- technical complexity must stay low
- examples must feel transferable to real work

## Repeated signals from inputs
- do not overload people with tools or interfaces
- avoid abstract examples with weak transfer
- show process, not only output
- preserve a strong wow moment
- end with one actionable participant outcome
- keep the path to next engagement visible

## Design tensions
1. wow vs practicality
2. openness vs structure
3. tool realism vs interface overload
4. mixed audience inclusivity vs advanced content
5. shared demo vs personal relevance

## Constraints
- 2-hour session
- low technical barrier
- live demo is desirable
- the first version must be ready fast
- infrastructure should stay minimal

## Open questions
- how much Codex / GitHub should participants touch directly?
- how much freedom should participants have in create-or-adapt practice?
- what is the right balance between shared case, mini-practice, and personal application?
